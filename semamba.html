SEMamba speech enhancement

Noise suppression for real call audio, judged by what it does for speech recognition.

In production, not released

Choosing the architecture

I compared Mamba-, Transformer- and Conformer-based designs for a model that had to run inside a real-time voice pipeline, and chose SEMamba: a Mamba model that enhances both magnitude and phase in the time-frequency domain.

Training

Parameters~1.88M
InitializationTrained from scratch
Data~5,000 hours of real-world call audio
