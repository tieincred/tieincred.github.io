VaaniCall

Speech recognition for phone calls in 10 Indian languages and English.

Model card on Hugging Face

Why phone audio

Most Indic speech models are trained on clean, wideband recordings. Contact-center audio is 8 kHz, compressed by codecs, clipped by voice-activity detection and full of line noise. General models that look strong on benchmarks fall apart on it, so VaaniCall is trained to expect it.

Training

  • Base: NVIDIA NeMo FastConformer-RNNT, fine-tuned from the VAANI checkpoint.
  • Phase 1, language alignment: encoder frozen; the decoder learns script and language mapping.
  • Phase 2, acoustic refinement: last five encoder layers and the full decoder unfrozen.
  • Data: IndicVoices and VAANI, 50,000 training clips per language.
  • Telephony simulator: 21 augmentation stages, including codecs, packet loss, handset EQ, VAD clipping, room impulse responses and real-world noise.
