Teacher-Student-Judge: synthetic preference data for training tool-using agents

Manuscript under review

About 8,000 DPO pairs were used to post-train Qwen3-30B-A3B, a Mixture-of-Experts model, on 8×H100 with FSDP and DeepSpeed ZeRO-3.

Training-free optimization of Mixture-of-Experts models

Fatima Research Institute for AI
Ongoing

Can an MoE model drop experts without retraining and keep its reasoning? I study expert pruning, router confidence, activation patterns and layer-level redundancy in OLMoE and DeepSeekMoE, with the goal of cutting inference cost while holding downstream task performance.

VaaniCall

Speech recognition, 11 Indian languages

A 430M-parameter FastConformer-RNNT model adapted for telephone audio. Trained in two stages on about 4.4 million utterances (40,000+ hours), with a 21-stage augmentation pipeline that simulates 8 kHz codecs, packet loss, VAD clipping, handsets and real-world noise.

SEMamba speech enhancement

Noise suppression for call audio

After comparing Mamba, Transformer and Conformer designs, I trained SEMamba from scratch (1.88M parameters, magnitude and phase enhancement) on about 5,000 hours of real call audio. I judged it by what it does for recognition: Whisper makes fewer errors on the cleaned audio. It beat MetricGAN+ and matched or exceeded MP-SENet under the same test. Runs in production; not released.

VoxCPM2 Indic

Text-to-speech, 12 languages

A full fine-tune of VoxCPM2 for Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu and code-switched English. The release includes the data preparation and balancing scripts, training code, and tests for hallucination, runaway generation and truncation.

In production

These models run inside the voice platform I built at Voicing AI as its first engineer. Cutting end-to-end response time from roughly 1.7–2.3 seconds to 650–850 ms took work on turn detection, streaming recognition and synthesis, and GPU serving with TensorRT, ONNX Runtime and vLLM. The platform runs in the cloud and on-premise for enterprise customers including Philips, Voya and Computershare.
