In production

These models run inside the voice platform I built at Voicing AI as its first engineer. Cutting end-to-end response time from roughly 1.7–2.3 seconds to 650–850 ms took work on turn detection, streaming recognition and synthesis, and GPU serving with TensorRT, ONNX Runtime and vLLM. The platform runs in the cloud and on-premise for enterprise customers including Philips, Voya and Computershare.
